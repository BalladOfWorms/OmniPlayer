## OmniPlayer v1.0.3

### Fixed

- **Grav'iton and Revenant Maiden are listed once.** Each appears a single time
  even when both of the game's copies are in your library.

---

## OmniPlayer v1.0.2

### Fixed

- **The search box shows letters in full.** Letters with tails, like j, g and y,
  are no longer cut off at the bottom.

### Improved

- **The Export window closes itself** once everything has exported, and the
  player shows where the files went. If anything fails to export, the window
  stays open so you can see why and try again.

---

## OmniPlayer v1.0.1

### Improved

- **Moving the window is smooth.** The whole player moves as one piece when you
  drag its title strip, with nothing trailing behind the mouse.
- **Snap support.** Dragging the player to the top of the screen maximises it,
  and dragging it to a side edge snaps it to half the screen, like any other
  Windows app. The mini player stays exactly where you put it.
